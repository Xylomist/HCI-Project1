A harbor-side pause

A place to warm up, cool down and take your time.

Harbor & Haze is a fictional sauna club imagined for a calmer kind of waterfront visit. Every part of the experience is designed to feel clear, welcoming and easy to navigate.

Explore sessionsPlan a first visit

Find your own rhythm

Move between spaces when it feels right. There is no set sequence, and taking a break is always an option.

01

Cedar sauna

A dry, gently lit room with warm cedar and a view toward the water. Start with a short stay and step out whenever you want.

About 80°C · quiet conversation

02

Cold plunge

Two supervised tubs at different temperatures. The plunge is optional; staff are nearby to explain the space and answer questions.

4°C and 10°C · staff nearby

03

Rest lounge

A lower-noise place to sit, cool down and enjoy tea. Rest here between activities or make it the main event.

Quiet zone · tea and water

The Harbor & Haze approach

Comfort comes before the routine.

We want it to be easy to understand your options and make a choice that suits you. Clear session details, simple booking, and friendly guidance make space for a visit that is your own.
