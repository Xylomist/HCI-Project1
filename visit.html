Common questions

What should I bring?

Just a swimsuit. We provide towels, lockers and tea.

Is the cold plunge safe for everyone?

Not if you have heart conditions or are pregnant. Check with your doctor first. Staff supervise every plunge.

Can I change my booking?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email up to 12 hours before for a free change.
