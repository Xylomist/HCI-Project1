Common questions

Still deciding? Here are a few quick answers.

What should I bring?

Just a swimsuit. We provide towels, lockers and tea.

Do I have to use the cold plunge?

No. Every activity is optional. You can skip the plunge, take a break or spend your visit in the lounge.

Is cold exposure right for me?

Cold exposure is not suitable for everyone. If you have a health concern, are pregnant or are unsure, check with a qualified healthcare professional before taking part. You can choose a session without a plunge.

Can I change my booking?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email up to 12 hours before for a free change.

I've never been to a sauna. Is that okay?

Absolutely. No experience is expected. Read our first-visit guide, or ask the team when you arrive.
