When you get here

We'll help you get oriented.

Check in with the team at the entrance. They'll show you where to change, where your locker is, and how to move between the sauna, plunge and lounge. Let them know if you'd like a slower introduction.

Good to know

Do I need to know how to use a sauna?

No. The team can explain the space and answer questions. You can step out or take a break at any time.

Do I have to try the cold plunge?

No. The plunge is optional, and there are sessions that don't include it. Choose what feels comfortable for you.

What if I have a health concern?

Sauna and cold exposure may not be suitable for everyone. If you have a health concern, are pregnant or are unsure, check with a qualified healthcare professional before taking part. You may skip any activity.

Can I ask for help with the space?

Yes. Contact the team before your visit or speak with us when you arrive. We'll do our best to answer questions and discuss what to expect.
